Sources and method

Advisories. CISA publishes ICS advisories in CSAF format at raw.githubusercontent.com/cisagov/CSAF. This page reads changes.csv for the newest advisory list, fetches the most recent set, and keeps those whose Critical infrastructure sectors note names Energy. Severity is the highest CVSS v3 or v4 base score across the advisory's vulnerabilities.

Regulatory. The Federal Register API, filtered to FERC with the term NERC, then narrowed to reliability-standard, CIP and bulk-power items. Paperwork Reduction Act collection notices are excluded — they dominate the raw results and carry no news.

CISA retired its RSS feeds in May 2025, including the KEV and advisory feeds. The CSAF repository is the machine-readable channel that replaced them.

Known limits

The KEV catalog is not cross-referenced here. CISA serves it without CORS headers, so a browser cannot read it from another origin — adding it would require a server-side step this page deliberately avoids. Advisories are drawn from the most recent set rather than the full year, so counts describe that window, not all of 2026. Sector classification is CISA's, reproduced as published, and an advisory can name several sectors.
